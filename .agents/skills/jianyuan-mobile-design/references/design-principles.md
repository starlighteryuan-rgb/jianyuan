# Design Principles

The visual philosophy of 见渊. Read this before choosing typography, spacing,
surfaces, color, or component language.

## Character

克制 (restrained), 安静 (quiet), 有呼吸感 (room to breathe), 留白 (whitespace),
低噪声 (low noise), 私密 (private), 人本 (human), 不判断 (non-judgmental),
不强迫用户行动 (never forces action), not over-AI, not an enterprise dashboard,
not productivity SaaS.

The product should feel like a quiet private place for a person's own thoughts,
not like a tool that evaluates them.

## Avoid

- neon AI aesthetics;
- purple/blue AI-cliché gradients;
- glassmorphism everywhere;
- gradients everywhere;
- cards everywhere;
- excessive shadow;
- excessive blur;
- high-saturation call-to-action buttons;
- gamification, streaks, scores;
- badge overload.

## Card is not the default answer

Ask first: **does this information actually need a Card container?** Prefer
whitespace, grouping, typography, dividers, timeline, spatial placement, and
subtle surface. Use a Card only when there is a genuine need for grouping,
hierarchy, or an interaction boundary.

## Awareness Has Three Distinct States

Awareness Main, New Awareness Result, and Awareness Open are different states
and must not share one object language.

- **Awareness Main:** stage, not object. It may keep a blurred circular / halo
  atmosphere and an attention center. It must not become a hard-edged circle,
  solid dark disc, bubble, or object shell.
- **New Awareness Result:** editorial text flow, not an object card. Keep the
  relation between “新的觉察”, the observation, and time clear. Use
  typography, whitespace, a very light focus line, and tonal emphasis.
  Do not wrap it in a circle, oval, halo, large shape, card, or floating panel.
- **Awareness Open:** keep the AB foreground reading logic. The opened
  observation enters the foreground; surrounding content recedes. Keep the
  reading order and response area calm. Do not rebuild it as a heavy card.

Never return to the regressed state of a track-shaped outline, a solid dark
circle, or text incorrectly enclosed by an object.

## Surfaces and depth

Depth should be quiet. Prefer subtle surface separation over stacked shadows and
heavy borders. A surface may separate a region without becoming a floating card.

## Color and state

Color is not the only carrier of state. Pair every state color with text, shape,
or position. Keep contrast usable in both light and dark mode. Avoid
high-saturation accents for the primary action; restraint reads as trust.

## Typography

Text is the primary material in 见渊, especially in Record and Understanding.
Favor a small number of stable scales. Do not shrink important user writing into
secondary metadata. Never lower information hierarchy to make a layout fit.

## Tone of the interface

The interface does not judge, diagnose, or push. It offers, and the user
decides. Copy and visuals should both reflect that: no verdict language, no
"your pattern is", no score, no streak.

## Semantics above style

AI Observation is a possibility; the user decides meaning. Reflection belongs to
the user. Never design a screen where an AI interpretation visually outranks the
user's own words. When style and semantics conflict, semantics win.
