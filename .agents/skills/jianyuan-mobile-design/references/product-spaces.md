# Product Spaces

The four spaces of 见渊 and what each one is for. Read this whenever designing
or auditing a mobile space.

## Record / 记录

**Meaning:** 我留下了什么.

**Visual keywords:** quiet, personal, direct, text-first, low cognitive load.

Future work may add Card redesign, tags, search, timeline, and grouping. But
Record must never read like an AI dashboard. It is the user's own words first;
everything else is secondary.

## Awareness / 觉察

**Meaning:** 有什么值得现在注意.

This is the most distinctive space. Keywords: surface, emergence, ripple,
spatial, transient attention.

It must not become an inbox, a notification center, a chat, a dashboard, or an
ordinary card feed. The core metaphor is: **觉察像是从已有经历中浮出来** —
awareness floats up out of existing experience.

### Awareness Main ≠ New Awareness Result ≠ Awareness Open

The three Awareness states are distinct and must not reuse one object language.

#### Awareness Main — Stage, Not Object

Main may keep a blurred circular / halo atmosphere, soft tonal diffusion, and
an attention center. It expresses “觉察正在发生的空间”.

Allowed: very light circular / concentric halo, soft tonal diffusion, low
contrast center focus, equivalent Light / Dark structure.

Forbidden: solid dark circular blocks, closed heavy outlines, obvious object
boundaries, and anything that reads as a card, ball, bubble, or object shell.

#### New Awareness Result — Observation Is Not Object

Awareness Result is editorial text flow, not an object card. It should read
like a discovered sentence, a surfaced observation, or a judgment clue worth
pausing for.

Prefer typography, whitespace, a very light focus line, tonal shift, and subtle
emergence. Keep the relation between “新的觉察”, the observation, and time
clear.

Do not use a circle, oval, halo, large shape, card, bubble, panel, or closed
container around the result.

#### Awareness Open — AB Foreground Reading Logic

Open keeps the AB foreground reading logic: the opened observation enters the
foreground, surrounding content recedes, reading order stays clear, and the
response area remains natural. Do not rebuild Open as a heavy card.

Never return to the regressed state of a track-shaped outline, a solid dark
circle, or text incorrectly enclosed by an object.

The accepted Bubble emergence timing remains frozen for automatic Awareness
entries. Do not redesign the accepted main structure without an explicit
request.

## Understanding / 理解

**Meaning:** 我自己留下的理解.

The content here belongs to the user. It should feel more stable than Awareness
and more sedimented than Record, and it must emphasize "this is my own
expression".

Never let AI Observation and User Reflection look identical here. The visual
language must keep "what the system suggested" and "what I decided" clearly
distinct.

The first-person anchor is frozen at `28 / 28`: it preserves the first-person
entry point without letting the drop cap outrank the body. Body text remains the
subject in both Light and Dark.

## Exploration / 探索

**Meaning:** 长期形成的结构与关系.

Keywords: relation, trajectory, structure, connection, longitudinal.

Exploration should not stay "just another group of cards" forever. It may
gradually become paths, relationships, clusters, and trajectories. But an AI
hypothesis must never be drawn as a confirmed fact.

## Shared vs distinct

Shared across all four spaces: typography family, spacing system, design tokens,
navigation, overall calm.

Distinct per space: Record falls, Awareness surfaces, Understanding sinks,
Exploration connects. Preserve this difference; do not produce one Card with
four titles.
